---
title: Maintenance plans
---

Enable **Recurring Plan** when adding maintenance to an item. Choose the interval
in calendar days, weeks, months, or years and set the first scheduled date.
Completing an occurrence creates the next one, measured from the actual completion
date. Month and year intervals clamp to the end of a shorter month: August 31 plus
six months is February 28 (or 29 in a leap year).

You can enter completed maintenance, its cost, and notes when creating a plan.
The completed record is kept and the next occurrence is scheduled automatically.
Converting an existing entry to a plan preserves its historical dates and cost.

Editing a completed record changes that record, not the plan's name, interval, or
next due date. Recurring history cannot be reopened while linked to a plan because
its successor may already exist. A standalone record can still be reopened.

Uncheck **Recurring Plan** to pause an existing plan. Paused plans keep their
history and pending occurrence, but send no maintenance reminders. Completing a
paused occurrence does not create another. Check the option again to resume;
if there is no pending occurrence, one is scheduled from the last completion.

## Different cartridge replacement intervals

For a water filter with two six-month cartridges, one annual cartridge and one
two-year cartridge, three plans can group the work into visits. If all cartridges
were replaced on January 1, 2026:

| Plan | First visit | Repeat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replace the two six-month cartridges | July 1, 2026 | Every year |
| Replace those two plus the annual cartridge | January 1, 2027 | Every two years |
| Replace all four cartridges | January 1, 2028 | Every two years |

The visits replace 2, 3, 2, then 4 cartridges, repeating every two years. These are
independent plans: a delayed completion shifts that plan's subsequent date. They
do not enforce a shared fixed calendar or a strict alternating sequence.

## API notes

- Entry updates replace the supplied entry fields, so empty notes, zero cost, and
  empty dates are meaningful. At least one of the scheduled/completed dates is
  required. Omit `planID` to retain the association; an explicit nil UUID detaches
  it. Use plan creation with `linkExistingEntryID` to convert a standalone entry.
- Plan creation accepts `completedDate` and `cost` for the first entry. When
  linking an existing entry, its own fields take precedence.
- An explicit `nextDueAt` update also moves the pending occurrence, without
  rewriting completed history. An interval change applies to future completions.
- Dates are calendar dates. The API accepts hour intervals only as whole days
  (multiples of 24); use day intervals in new clients. Intervals that overflow the
  supported four-digit calendar range are rejected.
- Plans and their entry associations are included in group export/import.
